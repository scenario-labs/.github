# Support

- **Using Scenario**: the [Help center](https://help.scenario.com) covers models,
  Creative Units and account settings.
- **Building on Scenario**: the [API documentation](https://docs.scenario.com) and
  the [MCP documentation](https://mcp.scenario.com/docs).
- **Account, billing, credits or API keys**: in-app support at
  [app.scenario.com](https://app.scenario.com) or
  [support@scenario.com](mailto:support@scenario.com). Keep these out of public
  issues.
- **A bug in an open-source repository**: open an issue in that repository, with
  the version, steps to reproduce, and what you expected.
- **A vulnerability**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

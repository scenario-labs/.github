[Scenario](https://www.scenario.com) is a creative AI platform for image, video, 3D and audio. Generate with the leading AI models, train your own, and automate the work with workflows and agents.

Start for free in the [app](https://app.scenario.com/login), hand the job to an agent through [Scenario MCP](https://mcp.scenario.com), or call it from your own pipeline with the [API](https://docs.scenario.com). Every surface has the same models, workflows and assets.

- 🤖 **Agents**: [Scenario MCP](https://mcp.scenario.com) for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and more · [Skills](https://github.com/scenario-labs/skills) · `npx skills add scenario-labs/skills`
- 🛠️ **Developers**: [API reference](https://docs.scenario.com) · [TypeScript SDK](https://docs.scenario.com/api/typescript) · [Python SDK](https://docs.scenario.com/api/python)
- 🎨 **Generate**: [Image](https://www.scenario.com/features/generate-images) · [Video](https://www.scenario.com/features/generate-videos) · [3D](https://www.scenario.com/features/generate-3d) · [Audio](https://www.scenario.com/features/generate-audio)
- 🧠 **Train**: [Custom models](https://www.scenario.com/features/train) · [Model composition](https://www.scenario.com/features/compose-models)
- ✂️ **Edit**: [Inpaint](https://www.scenario.com/features/retouch-inpaint) · [Remove background](https://www.scenario.com/features/remove-background) · [Upscale](https://www.scenario.com/features/enhance) · [Vectorize](https://www.scenario.com/features/vectorize) · [Pixelate](https://www.scenario.com/features/pixelate)
- 🔁 **Workflows**: [Workflows](https://www.scenario.com/features/workflows) · [Node Agent](https://www.scenario.com/features/node-agent) · [Genie](https://www.scenario.com/features/Scenario-genie)
- 🎮 **Plugins**: [Blender](https://github.com/scenario-labs/blender-plugin) · [Godot](https://github.com/scenario-labs/godot-plugin)
